A full program from kindergarten to grade 6

## App review notes (Apple "Notes" box and Google "App access")

No sign-in is needed. On first launch, tap "Add a kid", type any name and pick a grade.

The parents area (Me → For grown-ups) is protected by a parental gate: a multiplication question written in words (for example "fourteen times seven"), followed by the parent PIN. The first PIN is 1234, and the parent can change it there.

The microphone is only used in "I speak" practice, while the microphone button is on. The phone's own speech recognition turns the spoken word into text. No audio is recorded, stored or sent by the app.

Lesson audio clips are downloaded from our website (GitHub Pages). No personal data is sent.

Optional: in the parents area a parent can paste their own API key for an AI service (Anthropic, OpenAI or ElevenLabs). This is off by default, only reachable behind the parental gate, and the app works fully without it.
