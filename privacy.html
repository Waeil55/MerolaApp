Genius Kids Academy privacy policy

Applies to the Genius Kids Academy app for iPhone, iPad and Android, and the website waeil55.github.io/MerolaApp. Last updated 13 November 2026.

We do not collect, store or sell any information about children or parents. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Everything a child does in the app stays on that device.

What stays on the device

Kid profiles (a first name or nickname and an avatar), stars, levels, saved words, settings and the parent PIN are saved only in the app's storage on the device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app, or using Reset in the parents area, erases them.

Microphone and speech recognition

In the "I speak" practice a child can read a word aloud. Only while the microphone button is on, the phone's own speech recognition (Apple on iPhone and iPad, Google or the phone maker on Android) turns the spoken word into text so the app can say if it was right. The app does not record, save or send any audio. The phone maker's handling of speech recognition is described in their own privacy policies. The microphone is optional; a grown-up can check the word instead.

The reading voice

Lessons are read aloud with recorded voice clips. The app downloads each clip from our website (hosted by GitHub Pages) the first time it is needed and keeps it on the device. Like any website, GitHub may briefly log the device's internet address when a file is downloaded; we do not receive or use these logs. Text that has no recording is read by the phone's built-in voice, on the device.

Optional AI features for grown-ups

Out of the box the helper "Nova" gives built-in hints with no internet service. In the parents area, behind a grown-up question and the parent PIN, a parent may choose to add their own key for an AI service (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI or ElevenLabs). Only then, the questions typed to Nova and the text to be read aloud are sent to that company under the parent's own account and that company's privacy policy. Keys stay on the device and can be removed at any time. We never see them.

Children's privacy

The app is made for children from kindergarten to grade 6. It does not collect personal information from children, so it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR rules for children. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no social features and no links that take a child out of the app. The parents area is protected by a grown-up question and a PIN.
